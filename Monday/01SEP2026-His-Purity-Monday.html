4 There are different kinds of gifts, but the same Spirit distributes them. 5 There are different kinds of service, but the same Lord. 6 There are different kinds of working, but in all of them and in everyone it is the same God at work.

7 Now to each one the manifestation of the Spirit is given for the common good. 8 To one there is given through the Spirit a message of wisdom, to another a message of knowledge by means of the same Spirit, 9 to another faith by the same Spirit, to another gifts of healing by that one Spirit, 10 to another miraculous powers, to another prophecy, to another distinguishing between spirits, to another speaking in different kinds of tongues, and to still another the interpretation of tongues. 11 All these are the work of one and the same Spirit, and he distributes them to each one, just as he determines.

🧩 Unity in Diversity (v. 12–27)

Paul challenges two major spiritual temptations in the body of Christ: Inferiority ("I don't belong") and Superiority ("I don't need you").

🛡️ Defeating Inferiority

"If the foot should say, 'Because I am not a hand, I do not belong...'" (v. 15)

Core Truth: Your role is not your identity. Your identity is that you are a child of God. Unseen parts are indispensable.

⚖️ Defeating Superiority

"The eye cannot say to the hand, 'I don't need you!'" (v. 21)

Core Truth: No single person has all the gifts. God designed us to need each other so that there is no division in the body.

💬 Study & Discussion Questions

Click any question to view and record your answers or notes:

1. What unites all believers into one body? (v. 13) +

We were all baptized by one Spirit so as to form one body—whether Jews or Gentiles, slave or free—and given the one Spirit to drink.

2. Why start with confessing "Jesus is Lord" before gifts? +

Because the primary evidence of the Holy Spirit's work is allegiance to Jesus Christ as Lord, not spectacular abilities. The Lordship of Christ is the anchor for all spiritual gifts.

3. How does viewing gifts as "for others" change our mindset? +

Instead of asking "What am I good at?", we begin asking "How can God use this to bless or encourage someone else today?" It shifts our focus from performance to ministry.
