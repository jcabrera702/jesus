Every Believer Has a Gift. Every Gift Has a Purpose.

The Holy Spirit doesn't just accompany us—He empowers us. Through 1 Corinthians 12, discover how God gives abilities to make much of Jesus, serve others for the common good, and fulfill your unique role in the Body of Christ.

1 Corinthians 12:1–27 (NIV)

Key Scripture

1 Now about the gifts of the Spirit, brothers and sisters, I do not want you to be uninformed...

3 Therefore I want you to know that no one who is speaking by the Spirit of God says, “Jesus be cursed,” and no one can say, “Jesus is Lord,” except by the Holy Spirit.

4-7 There are different kinds of gifts, but the same Spirit distributes them... Now to each one the manifestation of the Spirit is given for the common good.

12-14 Just as a body, though one, has many parts, but all its many parts form one body, so it is with Christ. For we were all baptized by one Spirit so as to form one body...

26-27 If one part suffers, every part suffers with it; if one part is honored, every part rejoices with it. Now you are the body of Christ, and each one of you is a part of it.

Three Main Purposes of Spiritual Gifts

1. Make Much of Jesus

Spiritual gifts put the spotlight on Christ, not the gift-holder. The greatest miracle isn't extraordinary activity—it's the Spirit enabling you to declare and live "Jesus is Lord."

2. Serve Others

Gifts are not God's gift to you for self-enjoyment—they are God's gift through you for the common good. Hold your role loosely and your identity as a child of God tightly.

3. Play Your Part

Just like physical body parts or football team positions, every gift is essential. There is no room for feeling inferior or superior—every part is indispensable.

Interpretation Questions Q9 - Q19
Sermon Insight: Paul establishes that the clearest evidence of the Spirit's power isn't flashy spiritual activity—it's surrendering your heart to the Lordship of Jesus. Before discussing gifts, we must ask: Is Jesus my King calling the shots?
Sermon Insight: In John 16:14, Jesus notes that the Spirit glorifies Him. When gifts are used improperly, the person receives praise. When operated in the Spirit, the spotlight lands squarely on Jesus.
Sermon Insight: Pastor Seth emphasized: "Your identity is not your role. Your identity is that you are a child of God." Tying identity to a role makes us fragile if that role changes. Hold roles loosely, and let God hold you.
Sermon Insight: Just like a football team needs linemen as much as quarterbacks, the church needs every behind-the-scenes role (administration, changing trailer tires, hosting). Visible roles are not superior; every member is indispensable.
Application Questions Q20 - Q22

Shift Perspective: Stop asking "What am I good at?" and start asking "What in my life can make much of Jesus and help someone else?"

Practical Action: Look around tomorrow for someone who needs encouragement, practical help, or a listening ear. Put yourself and your abilities into God's hands and say: "God, here I am. Use me."
