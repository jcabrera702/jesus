Homework One

Revelation 12:1-2 — The Clothed Woman

Examine the identity of the heavenly woman in John's vision.

Scripture Context

"A great sign appeared in heaven: a woman clothed with the sun, with the moon under her feet and a crown of twelve stars on her head. She was pregnant and cried out in pain as she was about to give birth." — Revelation 12:1-2 (NIV)

Who is this Woman?

The identity of this woman is interpreted through two primary biblical perspectives. While both views carry historical symbolism, scripture strongly points toward the Nation of Israel as the complete prophetic representation.

Nation of Israel

Primary Interpretation: The imagery directly mirrors Joseph's dream in Genesis 37:9-10, where the sun represents Jacob, the moon represents Rachel, and the 12 stars represent the 12 tribes of Israel.

Israel is pictured throughout the Old Testament as the "wife of Yahweh" who groans in birth pangs to bring forth the Messiah (Micah 5:2-3, Isaiah 26:17-18).

The Virgin Mary

Secondary/Literal Aspect: Mary is the historical individual who physically gave birth to Jesus Christ (the male child of Rev 12:5). However, Mary herself stems from Israel. Most biblical scholars view Mary as a personification of corporate Israel rather than the sole fulfillment of this symbolic prophecy.

Supporting Scriptures

"Then he had another dream... 'Listen,' he said, 'I had another dream, and this time the sun and moon and eleven stars were bowing down to me.'" — Genesis 37:9 (Imagery of Israel's 12 Tribes)
"As a pregnant woman approaches the time to give birth and writhes and cries out in her pain, so were we in your presence, LORD." — Isaiah 26:17 (Israel's Birth Pangs)
Homework Two

Identification of Biblical Entities

Understanding God's distinct groups and their scriptural titles.

1. The Nation of Israel

Definition: The physical descendants of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob with whom God established an everlasting covenant.

Alternative Scriptural Names:
The Chosen People (Deut 7:6) The Apple of His Eye (Zech 2:8) My Firstborn Son (Exodus 4:22) House of Jacob (Luke 1:33)
"For you are a people holy to the LORD your God. The LORD your God has chosen you out of all the peoples on the face of the earth to be his people, his treasured possession." — Deuteronomy 7:6

2. The Church (Ekklesia)

Definition: The body of believers (both Jews and Gentiles) redeemed through faith in Jesus Christ, called out from the world.

Alternative Scriptural Names:
The Body of Christ (1 Cor 12:27) The Bride of Christ (2 Cor 11:2) Household of God (Eph 2:19) A Royal Priesthood (1 Peter 2:9)
"Now you are the body of Christ, and each one of you is a part of it." — 1 Corinthians 12:27

3. Gentiles (Goyim / Ethne)

Definition: Non-Jewish nations or individuals who historically were outside the Jewish covenant, but are now offered salvation through Christ.

Alternative Scriptural Names:
The Nations (Psalm 2:8) Uncircumcision (Ephesians 2:11) Wild Olive Shoot (Romans 11:17) Heathen (Galatians 3:8 - KJV)
"Therefore, remember that formerly you who are Gentiles by birth... remember that at that time you were separate from Christ, excluded from citizenship in Israel..." — Ephesians 2:11-12
Homework Three

Revelation 12:3 — The Great Red Dragon

Examine the identity, symbolism, and lessons regarding the adversary.

Scripture Context

"Then another sign appeared in heaven: an enormous red dragon with seven heads and ten horns and seven crowns on its heads." — Revelation 12:3

Who is the Red Dragon?

Scripture removes all guesswork regarding this identity. Revelation 12:9 explicitly states: "The great dragon was hurled down—that ancient serpent called the devil, or Satan, who leads the whole world astray."

Key Symbolism & Lessons Learned:

  • Identity: Satan / The Devil

    Confirmed explicitly in Rev 12:9. He is the ultimate spiritual adversary of God, Israel, and the Church.

  • Red Color: Murder & Violence

    The color red signifies his murderous nature. Jesus stated in John 8:44 that Satan "was a murderer from the beginning."

  • 7 Heads & 10 Horns: World Powers & Authority

    Represents vast political intelligence (heads), raw power/strength (horns), and dominion over earthly kingdom systems (crowns).
