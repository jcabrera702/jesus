Sermon Overview & Notes

Partnering with the Holy Spirit

Shane Philip | The Crossing Church Las Vegas

01

He Counsels Us

The Holy Spirit teaches, reminds, and guides us into truth. Unlike unsolicited advice that walks away, a true counselor helps you see what is true and stays to help you live in it.

John 14:26, 16:13
02

He Convicts Us

Conviction is not condemnation. Condemnation says you are a failure and pushes God away. Conviction draws you back by revealing areas that don't reflect who God made you to be.

John 16:8, Romans 8:1
03

He Comforts Us

The Spirit doesn't wait until you clean yourself up—He comes alongside you directly in your pain, grief, and anxiety so you never carry heavy burdens alone.

John 14:16
04

He Intercedes For Us

When you are too broken or overwhelmed to form words, the Spirit lifts the heavy load and pleads your case before God through wordless groans according to His perfect will.

Romans 8:26-27
05

He Confirms Our Identity

When shame or fear whispers lies, the Holy Spirit speaks back to remind us: you are not an orphan—you are a loved, forgiven, and adopted child of God.

Romans 8:15-16

Key Term: Paraclete (Paracletos)

A Greek ancient warrior term referring to a battle partner called to your side to cover your blind side while fighting back-to-back. The Holy Spirit is sent not merely to reside near us, but to cover our blind spots in life's battles.

The Speeding Ticket Illustration

Rushing home with paint while paying a crew by the hour, Pastor Shane got pulled over for driving 45 mph in a 35 mph zone—right in front of the police station. Being consumed with the pressure of the moment made him oblivious to what was right beside him.

"I wonder if that's a picture of how many of us live the Christian life. Not rebellious, not unbelieving, just unaware... We live as though we are completely on our own."

This past Easter, I told the story about our painting disaster. We were painting the inside of our house and what I thought was a natural linen color looked a whole lot more like peach. And I'm like, "We cannot leave the walls of our house peach." So, we jumped in my car and we headed to the paint store to pick out another color. Meanwhile, I had an entire paint crew at my house getting paid to wait for me. Every minute I was gone was costing me money. So, I was moving as fast as I could. We pick out a new color and we start heading back from Rainbow going down Windmill and all I'm thinking about is paint and how much money that this is going to cost me. And about that time, I look in my rearview mirror and I see flashing lights...
