Galatians 5 Walkthrough

His Purity & The Gardener

Understanding how the Holy Spirit produces real transformation from the inside out.

🍋 The Lemon Tree Metaphor

You are the gardener, not the fruit source. No matter how many times you tell a tree to grow fruit, nothing happens by force. The gardener prepares soil, waters, pulls weeds, and protects the tree. God produces the fruit.

"So I say, walk by the Spirit, and you will not gratify the desires of the flesh." Galatians 5:16

⚠️ Works vs. Fruit

Works of the Flesh: What we manufacture out of broken human nature (e.g., rage, jealousy, selfishness). Nobody has to teach us how to grow weeds.


Fruit of the Spirit: What the Spirit produces in us as we walk with Him. It is singular—one fruit with nine beautiful characteristics.

🛡️ Identity Precedes Behavior

You don't become a child of God by acting like one; you live like a child of God because you already are one. Behavior flows from identity.

"I have been crucified with Christ and I no longer live, but Christ lives in me." Galatians 2:20

🚶 Keeping in Step Daily

Walking with the Spirit isn't a single sprint—it is thousands of ordinary steps taken every day. Use these daily steps to stay aligned.

1
Morning Alignment

Ask: "Holy Spirit, where are you leading today? What conversations do you want me to have?"

2
Mid-Day Pause

In tense moments (e.g., Monday morning conversations): Listen to the Spirit's prompt before reacting.

3
Evening Reflection

Identify where you kept step vs. where you lagged or ran ahead.
