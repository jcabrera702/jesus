One Body, Many Roles

Three Purposes of Spiritual Gifts

1

Make Much of Jesus

The ultimate goal of spiritual gifts is not to bring attention or praise to ourselves, but to shine a spotlight directly on Jesus Christ as King and Lord.

  • The greatest miracle is surrendering to Jesus as Lord.
  • John 16:14: The Spirit always glorifies Christ.
2

Serve Others

Spiritual gifts aren't God's gifts *to* you for personal status—they are God's gifts *through* you for the common good and benefit of others.

  • Your identity is in being a child of God, not your role.
  • Hold positions loosely; let God hold you tightly.
3

Play Your Part in the Body

Just like a sports team or human body, every role is indispensable. No single part can do everything, and every position contributes to the goal.

  • Unique gifts eliminate rivalry and competition.
  • You are the hands, feet, and compassion of Jesus today.

Sermon Illustrations: Gifts in Action

Administration

Organizing details, logistics, and planning events so the vision moves forward smoothly.

Hospitality

Opening your home, creating welcoming environments, and caring for guests generously.

Practical Service

Using technical skills, fixing trailer tires, logistics, and step-by-step physical help.

Creative Arts

Design, media, and story-telling that capture what God is doing and share it with others.

Reflection & Action Steps

Instead of asking "What am I good at?", ask: "What in my life can make much of Jesus and serve others?"

Discussion Questions

Question 1

Have you ever felt like your contribution in family, work, or church didn't matter? How does 1 Corinthians 12 challenge that feeling?

Question 2

What is the difference between performing a role for personal identity versus letting God use you as an instrument in His hand?

Question 3

Who is someone you can encourage or serve this coming week using the unique position God has placed you in?
