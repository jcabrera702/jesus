Stop trying to live the Christian life on your own power. Learn what it means to rely on the multiplying force of the Holy Spirit.

Core Pillars

1. You Are Never Alone
God's invisible, personal presence lives in you. You never walk into a hospital room, difficult conversation, or grief alone.
2. You Are Never Powerless
The same Spirit that raised Jesus from the dead dwells in you, giving power over addiction, temptation, and unforgiveness.
3. You Are Never Without Hope
The Holy Spirit is God's down payment (guarantee) for eternity, allowing you to experience heaven’s presence right now.

Theological Insights

Hebrew & Greek Definitions

Ruach (Hebrew): Breath, wind, or spirit.

Pneuma (Greek): Breath, wind, or spirit (Root word for pneumatic or pneumonia).

Old vs. New Covenant Spirit
  • Old Testament: Temporary empowering of select leaders (e.g., Joseph, Bezalel, David).
  • In Jesus: God tabernacled (pitched his tent) with mankind.
  • New Covenant: Permanent indwelling in every believer post-Pentecost.
Understanding the Trinity
One God existing in three distinct persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit. The Spirit is a Person to be known, not an impersonal force.
