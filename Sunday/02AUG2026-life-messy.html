Three Pillar Lessons

Core principles extracted from Genesis 26

Pillar 01

Trust God's Promises

In famine, Isaac planned to flee to Egypt, but God told him to stay. God didn't instantly stop the famine; He promised: "I will be with you."

Scripture: Gen 26:1–5
Pillar 02

Live No Lies

Driven by fear, Isaac claimed Rebekah was his sister. Carrying deception requires managing heavy secrets. True freedom comes when we bring our "junk out loud" into God's light.

Scripture: Proverbs 28:13
Pillar 03

Walk in the Way of Jesus

When Philistines blocked Isaac's wells, he didn't retaliate with power. He kept moving and digging until God made room (Rehoboth). Love, not force, is our marker.

Scripture: John 13:34–35

Isaac's Three Wells

How Isaac navigated conflict when others blocked his blessings

Well #1 Conflict

Esek

"Dispute"

The herders of Gerar argued that the water belonged to them. Isaac didn't fight back; he named the reality and moved forward.

Well #2 Opposition

Sitna

"Opposition / Accusation"

They dug a second well and quarreled over that one too. Rather than taking matters into his own hands, Isaac let go again.

Well #3 Breakthrough

Rehoboth

"Room to Flourish"

No one quarreled over this well. Isaac declared: "Now the Lord has given us room, and we will flourish."
