Series: Trusting the Promise

Following God’s Lead

Genesis 24 • Navigating life's unknown canvas when you can't see the whole picture.

The Big Picture

When you want to trust God with your family, career, or future, it would be nice if you could just see the plan. But God often works from a bigger canvas while we only see a small snapshot.

Primary Passage: Genesis 24 [01:53]
Background: Abraham is old [04:01], Sarah recently passed away at 127 years old [01:57], and Isaac is a 37-year-old eligible bachelor [02:05]. Abraham sends his trusted senior household servant back to find a wife for Isaac [04:09] devoted to the one true God [05:07].
"God's will is so much more about who you are becoming than where you go or what you do."

Core Principles & Lessons

1

Filter Decisions Through God's Word

Abraham made a deliberate plan [07:26], but refused to force outcomes, manipulate circumstances, or compromise on what mattered [08:01]. When waiting on God feels difficult, it's easy to take matters into our own hands (like Hagar earlier) [07:45]. Instead, we must filter every decision through His wisdom and release the outcomes [09:03].

Scripture Focus: Genesis 24:6-8 [06:01] • Trusting that God's ways are best even when the full plan is unclear [07:21].
2

Pray Before You Proceed

Upon arriving at the destination, the servant stopped at the well outside town and explicitly invited God into the expedition through prayer before making any moves [10:35].

Scripture Focus: Genesis 24:12-14 [10:41] • Prayer changes things, aligns our hearts with God's will, and opens doors for His work rather than relying solely on our own strength [11:38].
3

Keep Doing the Next Right Thing

Rebekah didn't know a massive covenant story was unfolding [15:41]. She simply saw a thirsty traveler and took on the grueling task of drawing hundreds of gallons of water for his camels one jar at a time [15:17].

Scripture Focus: Genesis 24:18-20 [14:17] • Focus on everyday integrity, character development, and diligence in the present rather than just chasing final destinations [16:59].
4

Look for God's Fingerprints

When Laban and Bethuel heard the testimony of how God orchestrated every step, they concluded: *"This is from the Lord"* [22:04].

Scripture Focus: Genesis 24:50-51 [22:04] • Shift from only looking for direct answers to actively noticing God's ongoing activity and presence behind the scenes [22:49].
5

Give a Brave Yes to God

When asked if she would leave home to marry an unknown man in an unfamiliar land, Rebekah chose to trust God and said, *"I will go"* [24:58].

Scripture Focus: Genesis 24:58 [24:58] • Stop demanding absolute clarity as an excuse to avoid risk; walk to the edge of what you know and take one more step of faith [26:31].

Weekly Reflection

Where do you need to stop waiting for full guarantees and take a step of trust this week?
